### R1. Swym Wishlist Plus

**Núcleo:** Wishlist for shoppers (multiple and anonymous lists, sharing) with email/SMS automations for reminders, restock and price-drop alerts, POS access for staff and shopper behavior reports.

**Construibilidad: S.** Wishlist button and page via theme app extension, anonymous lists merged on login, product/inventory webhooks for alerts and Klaviyo events are well-trodden; POS and customer account extensions add little extra effort.

**Qué se mejoraría:**

- Free or low-cost entry plan (Swym starts at $29.99/month with no free tier)
- Built-in back-in-stock and price-drop emails without requiring an ESP
- Wishlist demand analytics to guide restocking and discounts
- Gift registry mode for weddings and baby showers

### R2. Loox ‑ Product Reviews App

**Núcleo:** Collects photo and video product reviews automatically (emails, QR codes, discounts) and shows them in on-brand visual widgets, with AI sorting, highlights, translations and syndication to Shop App, Google and Meta.

**Construibilidad: M.** Visual review collection, media storage, request emails and widgets fit in 2-3 months; syndication to Google/Meta/TikTok and AI features add time but are not blockers for an MVP.

**Qué se mejoraría:**

- Much cheaper paid plan than $49.99/month with no per-order overage
- Flat unlimited pricing for growing stores
- Video review collection optimized for mobile with in-browser recording
- Free import and migration from Loox to lower switching cost

### R3. Appikon ‑ Back In Stock

**Núcleo:** Collects waitlist signups on out-of-stock products and automatically sends back-in-stock email/SMS alerts on restock, with demand reporting.

**Construibilidad: S.** Signup widget, inventory webhooks, email and SMS sending via providers like Twilio, and basic demand reports fit in about 4 weeks for one developer.

**Qué se mejoraría:**

- Cheaper paid entry than $19.99/month
- Predictable SMS pricing instead of $0.05-$0.40 per message by length
- Klaviyo/Shopify Email sync of waitlist contacts
- Restock prioritization report ranking products by waitlist demand

### R4. BOOSTER AI SEO + AEO OPTIMIZER

**Núcleo:** Continuously scans the store and automatically fixes SEO issues: image compression, broken links, meta tags and structured data, plus LLMs.txt and AI-oriented meta for visibility in ChatGPT/Gemini.

**Construibilidad: M.** Scanning, image optimization, broken-link redirects, AI meta generation and LLMs.txt are individually simple, but doing them automatically and safely across themes and large catalogs is a 1-3 month build in a crowded market.

**Qué se mejoraría:**

- Lower entry price than $39/month
- Clear audit log and one-click undo of every automatic change
- Measurement of AI-assistant visibility (mentions in ChatGPT/Gemini answers)
- Explain fixes in plain language for non-technical merchants

**Construibilidad: M.** Each piece (image compression pipeline, AI meta/ALT generation, JSON-LD, redirect management, audits) is standard, but covering them all reliably across themes and large catalogs is a 1-3 month effort in a very crowded category.

**Qué se mejoraría:**

- Cheaper entry plan than $34.95/month for small catalogs
